Mathematics, rebuilt from the foundations.

Not just learning more. Understanding better.

MCP Online is Chaos Theory's live online maths programme designed to help children rebuild the mathematical foundations they may have missed along the way.

Instead of placing children simply by school grade, the programme begins with a free trial class and diagnostic.

This helps identify the exact point where a child's mathematical understanding needs repair, allowing them to begin at the level that is right for them.

"The goal isn't to push children ahead. It's to make sure the foundations beneath them are strong."

How MCP Online works

A structured path from diagnosis to confidence.

01

Free Trial + Diagnostic

Children begin with a free trial class and diagnostic to identify the areas where their mathematical foundations need strengthening.

02

Find the Right Level

Children are placed into the appropriate level based on their actual understanding rather than simply their school grade.

03

Learn in Small Groups

Live classes are conducted twice a week in small groups of up to 8 students, allowing for focused and personalised support.

04

Track Progress

Regular progress checks and parent reports help families understand how the child's mathematical understanding is developing.

05

Final Assessment

A final assessment demonstrates what has changed throughout the programme.

06

Completion Certificate

Children receive a completion certificate after completing their level.
