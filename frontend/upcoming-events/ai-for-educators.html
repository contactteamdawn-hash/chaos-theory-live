AI for Educators

Not just using AI. Teaching with purpose.

AI for Educators is a live, two-hour online workshop designed to help teachers move beyond experimenting with AI and start using it meaningfully in the classroom.

Through live demonstrations and hands-on activities, participants will explore AI tools for lesson design, differentiated learning, assessment, feedback, remediation and classroom communication.

The workshop also addresses ethical AI use, including academic honesty, student data privacy, bias and setting clear expectations for students.

DATE 16 August 2026
TIME 4:00 – 6:00 PM IST
FORMAT Live Online

What you'll explore

  • AI for lesson design
  • Differentiated learning
  • Assessment and feedback
  • Remediation and classroom communication
  • Ethical and responsible AI use
  • Academic honesty and student data privacy
  • Bias and setting clear expectations for students
Every module is practical, with participants creating something they can use in their own teaching.
No prior experience with AI tools is required.
